DXWhisp transforms your voice into action. Record meetings, ideas, or quick thoughts — and let AI do the heavy lifting.

■ SMART TRANSCRIPTION
Your recordings are automatically transcribed using Apple's speech recognition — fast and accurate. Edit transcriptions in-app and re-extract insights without losing your progress.

■ SPEAKER DETECTION
DXWhisp detects speaker turns in conversations, labeling who said what with up to 6 speakers. Perfect for meetings and interviews.

■ AI-POWERED INSIGHTS
Action items, dates, events, and key points are extracted automatically using Natural Language Processing, enhanced by on-device AI.
- Action items → Reminders
- Dates & events → Calendar
- Key points → Summary

■ SEAMLESS INTEGRATION
Export action items directly to Apple Reminders and events to Apple Calendar. Enable auto-export in Settings and everything happens automatically when you finish recording.

■ TAGS & ORGANIZATION
Organize notes with colored tags, filter by tag or search by text, favorite important notes, and use bulk operations to manage multiple notes at once.

■ PRIVATE & SECURE
Your voice notes are protected with complete file encryption. Lock sensitive notes behind Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID — biometric authentication keeps your data safe.

■ BEAUTIFUL & SIMPLE
A clean, minimal interface with a guided onboarding experience. A live animation responds to your voice while recording, making the experience feel alive.

Perfect for:
- Meeting notes with speaker attribution
- Lecture recordings
- Quick ideas and brainstorms
- Voice journaling
- Interview transcripts

DXWhisp requires iOS 26 or later.
